## What the Pexels License allows

- Free use, including commercial use, with no attribution required (credit is given here
  anyway, because the photographers deserve it).
- Modifying the photos. The files here were cropped to their frames and re-encoded.

What it does **not** allow:

- Selling unaltered copies of the photos, for example as prints or stock.
- Redistributing them on other stock or wallpaper platforms.
- Implying that people or brands in a photo endorse your product.

Shipping them inside this free, open-source template as demo content is ordinary use.
**For a real store, replace them with photography of your own pieces.** Keep the filenames
(each is the product's slug) and nothing else needs to change. See `CUSTOMIZATION.md` §6.

Photos were chosen to show no third-party brand names or logos. If you swap any of them,
check yours for the same thing before launch.
