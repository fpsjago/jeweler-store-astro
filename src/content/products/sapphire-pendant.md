## Royal Sapphire Pendant

A mesmerizing 3-carat pear-shaped Ceylon sapphire captures the essence of the sky at midnight, framed by a glittering double halo of pavé diamonds and finished with a single pearl drop. This classic pendant design showcases the sapphire's legendary "royal blue" color in a setting worthy of its regal heritage.

### The Ceylon Sapphire

Sri Lanka (formerly Ceylon) has been the world's premier source of fine blue sapphires for over 2,000 years. Ceylon sapphires are prized for their pure, vivid blue color with excellent transparency. Unlike sapphires from some other sources, Ceylon stones exhibit a crystalline clarity that allows light to dance through the stone.

### The Royal Blue

The term "royal blue" refers to the most desirable sapphire color – a pure, vivid blue with medium to medium-dark tone and strong saturation. The sapphire in this pendant sits at the deep end of that range, a velvety navy with remarkable consistency throughout the stone. In different lighting conditions, you'll see subtle shifts from midnight to deep royal blue.

### Double Halo Design

The two rows of pavé diamonds serve multiple design purposes:

1. **Magnification**: Optically enlarges the sapphire's appearance
2. **Brilliance**: Adds sparkle that complements the sapphire's color
3. **Protection**: Provides a buffer protecting the sapphire's edges
4. **Versatility**: Makes the piece suitable for both day and evening wear

### Care Instructions

- **Daily Wear**: Sapphires are extremely durable (9 on Mohs scale)
- **Cleaning**: Wipe pearls with a soft damp cloth; never use ultrasonic cleaners on pearls
- **Storage**: Store flat in an individual pouch to protect the pearls
- **Professional Care**: Annual inspection to ensure settings are secure
- **Activities**: Safe to wear during most activities, remove during heavy physical work

### Unheated & Natural

This sapphire is unheated and untreated – its extraordinary color is entirely natural. Unheated Ceylon sapphires represent less than 5% of sapphires on the market and command premium prices for their rarity and pure, natural beauty.
