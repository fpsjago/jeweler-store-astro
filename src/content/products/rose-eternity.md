### Yellow Gold Warmth

Yellow gold has never left fine jewelry design. Alloyed for strength while keeping its rich color, 14K yellow gold offers:

- **Warmth**: The golden hue complements all skin tones beautifully
- **Durability**: The 14K alloy is harder wearing than higher-karat gold
- **Versatility**: Pairs beautifully with white gold, rose gold, and platinum
- **Timelessness**: Both vintage-inspired and thoroughly modern

### Garland Claw Setting

Each marquise sits at a slight angle between two round diamonds, creating the look of a continuous garland of leaves. Every diamond is held securely in its own set of claws, providing:

- **Maximum brilliance**: Less metal, more diamond visible
- **Secure hold**: Each stone is held by its own claws
- **Comfortable fit**: Smooth, low-profile design
- **Durability**: Engineered for everyday wear

### Care Instructions

- **Daily Wear**: Remove during heavy physical activity
- **Cleaning**: Soak in warm soapy water, brush gently with soft toothbrush
- **Professional Care**: Inspect settings every 6-12 months
- **Rhodium Plating**: Not required (yellow gold doesn't need plating)
- **Storage**: Store flat to maintain shape
