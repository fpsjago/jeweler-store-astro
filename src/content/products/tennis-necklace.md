## Diamond Lattice Collar

The ultimate expression of timeless luxury: pavé and baguette diamonds of exceptional quality, each precisely matched and set in an openwork platinum lattice. A sweeping central motif gathers around a round brilliant and ends in a pear-shaped drop, delivering breathtaking brilliance at the base of the neck.

### The Collar Form

Where a line necklace is a single row of stones, a collar is built as a surface. Here the diamonds sit in an open lattice that lets light pass through the metal, so the piece reads as lace rather than a chain while still lying flat and close to the neck.

### Precision Matching

Creating a flawless diamond collar requires exceptional skill and patience. Each diamond in the lattice must be:

1. **Size-matched**: Within 0.01ct of adjacent stones
2. **Color-matched**: Identical color grade throughout
3. **Cut-matched**: Uniform depth and proportions
4. **Brilliance-matched**: Consistent sparkle and fire

Our gemologists evaluate hundreds of diamonds to assemble a perfectly matched set for each necklace. The process can take weeks to achieve the seamless flow of light this piece exhibits.

### Setting Technique

The pavé diamonds are bead-set into the lattice and the baguettes are channel-set along the sweep. This technique offers:

- **Security**: Beads and channels hold each stone firmly
- **Flexibility**: Hinged sections let the collar move naturally with the body
- **Brilliance**: Maximum light exposure from all angles
- **Durability**: Each setting is independently secure
- **Repairability**: Individual settings can be serviced without affecting others
