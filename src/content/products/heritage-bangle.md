## Heritage Bangle

A celebration of ornamental design and traditional goldsmith techniques, the Heritage Bangle is a statement piece that bridges past and present. Crafted from solid 18K yellow gold and adorned with filigree, granulation and small set stones, each bangle is a unique work of wearable art.

### Ornamental Inspiration

Traditional goldsmithing has long favored rich surface work: twisted wire, fine granulation, and stones set into the metal itself. Our Heritage Bangle pays homage to that ornamental tradition while incorporating modern comfort and wearability.

### Master Goldsmith's Touch

Each Heritage Bangle passes through the skilled hands of our master goldsmith, who has practiced this rare art form for over 40 years. Using traditional tools, they twist the rope borders, apply the filigree and granulation, and set each small stone by hand. The process takes approximately 8-10 hours per piece, ensuring each bangle is truly one-of-a-kind.

### The Pattern

The pattern features repeating ornamental motifs bordered by twisted gold rope. Scrolls of fine wire, tiny granules of gold, and small faceted pale stones interlock in a rhythmic dance around the bangle. The textured filigree contrasts beautifully with the high-polish gold rims that frame it.

### Wearing & Styling

The Heritage Bangle makes a bold statement worn alone or can be stacked with other bangles for a more dramatic look. The 12mm width provides substantial presence on the wrist without sacrificing comfort. The hinged opening allows for easy on and off, while the secure clasp ensures worry-free wear.

**Styling Suggestions:**
- Pair with a cocktail ring for evening elegance
- Stack with thinner bangles in mixed metals for modern contrast
- Wear as an heirloom piece for special occasions
- Perfect with both contemporary and vintage-inspired wardrobes

### Care Instructions

- **Cleaning**: Wipe with a soft, lint-free cloth after wear
- **Deep Cleaning**: Soak in warm soapy water, gently brush filigree areas with a soft toothbrush
- **Polishing**: Professional polishing every 2-3 years to maintain high shine
- **Storage**: Store in individual soft pouch to prevent scratches
- **Avoid**: Impact, harsh chemicals, chlorinated water
