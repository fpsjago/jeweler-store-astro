## Artisan Gold Pendant

A masterpiece of traditional goldsmithing, this handcrafted 18K gold medallion showcases intricate engraving that represents centuries of artisan tradition. ### The Medallion Tradition

The engraved gold medallion is one of the oldest forms of personal jewelry, worn as a token, a seal and a keepsake. This enduring art form requires exceptional skill, patience, and artistic vision to cut fine, legible detail into a small disc of gold.

### The Artisan's Process

Creating this pendant requires mastery of traditional techniques that few modern jewelers still practice:

**1. Blank Preparation (2-3 hours)**
- An 18K gold disc is cut and domed to a gentle curve
- A raised rim is formed around the edge
- The surface is annealed to the perfect workability

**2. Engraving (8-12 hours)**
- The twelve zodiac signs are cut into the border by hand
- The central figure is engraved without templates
- Each segment is checked under magnification

**3. Assembly & Soldering (4-5 hours)**
- The bail and rim are joined using micro-soldering techniques
- Every joint is cleaned and refined by hand
- Precise heat control protects the engraved detail

**4. Finishing (2-3 hours)**
- Hand-polishing to highlight dimensional depth
- Selective oxidation to enhance pattern definition
- Final quality inspection and hallmarking
